## 6. Rules to keep the signals consistent

1. Always write the name as **GymHopp** — capital G, capital H. Not "gymhopp" and
   not "Gym Hopp". Exception: the email address and the Instagram handle, which are
   lowercase by design.
2. Always link to `https://gymhopp.online/` with the `https://` and the trailing `/`.
3. Always give the price as "from ₹60" and note that each gym sets its own price.
4. Always describe it as a "gym day pass" or "day pass", never as a membership.
5. Always name Davanagere as the launch city.
6. Do not describe GymHopp as related to hula hoops, hooping or hoop fitness
   equipment in any listing.
