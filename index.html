About GymHopp

What Is GymHopp?

GymHopp is a gym marketplace for flexible, pay-per-visit access.

A normal gym contract asks you to pay every month, whether you train or not. GymHopp replaces that with a gym day pass: you pick a partner gym, pay for the one day you want to train, and check in with a QR pass on your phone.

It is built for people who move around — business travellers, students home for the holidays, tourists and anyone new to a city who needs a gym for a few days rather than a full year.

FAQ

GymHopp, Answered

What is GymHopp?

GymHopp is a gym marketplace for flexible access. It helps you find partner gyms and buy a day pass so you can train for the days you are in town, without signing up for a monthly membership.

What is a gym day pass?

A gym day pass gives you access to one gym for one day. You pay once for your visit, check in at the gym and train. A day pass is not a membership, it does not renew, and there is nothing to cancel.

Can I visit a gym without buying a monthly membership?

Yes. GymHopp exists for exactly that. You buy a single-day pass instead of a monthly membership, so you only pay for the days you actually train.

How is GymHopp different from a traditional gym membership?

A traditional membership is a recurring monthly commitment to one gym. GymHopp is pay-per-visit: you buy a day pass for the gym and date you want, with no joining fee, no minimum commitment and no cancellation call.

How do I find a gym on GymHopp?

GymHopp lists partner gyms by city, with the facilities, location and day-pass price for each one. GymHopp is launching first in Davanagere with selected partner gyms, so join the early-access list and we will send you the partner list when it goes live.

How do payment and booking work?

When booking is open in your city, you choose a gym and date, pay for that single day pass online, and receive a QR pass on your phone. We are onboarding our first partner gyms in Davanagere, so the earliest way to get in is the early-access list.

How does QR check-in work?

After you pay for a day pass, GymHopp generates a QR pass on your phone. You show it at the gym's front desk, staff scan it, and the system confirms the pass is paid and valid for that day before you train.

What do gym owners get from GymHopp?

Partner gyms earn extra revenue from day-pass visitors who would otherwise skip a workout. You set your own day-pass price, day passes sit alongside your existing memberships, and payouts are settled weekly to your bank account from the partner dashboard.

Where is GymHopp available?

GymHopp is launching first in Davanagere, Karnataka, India, with selected partner gyms. Early access is open now, and more cities will follow as we add partner gyms.

How do I get started with GymHopp?

Gym-goers can join the early-access list on the GymHopp homepage with their email. Gym owners can apply to become a partner gym from the For Gyms page, and we will help set up your gym before day passes go live.
