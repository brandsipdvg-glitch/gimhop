The company

What Is GymHopp?

GymHopp is a gym day-pass platform built to close the gap between how people actually move through a city and how gyms actually sell access.

GymHopp lets you pick a gym and a date, pay for that single day online, and receive a QR pass on your phone. You show it at the front desk, staff scan it, and the system confirms the pass before you train.

For gym owners, it is revenue that does not exist today: visitors who would otherwise never walk through the door, at a price the gym sets itself, with weekly settlements.

Our tagline, "Pay only for the days you train", reflects what we're building — access that flexes with your life instead of locking you into a contract.

  • Flexible access — buy a single day pass, not a monthly commitment
  • Instant QR pass — arrives on your phone as soon as payment is confirmed
  • Verified check-in — staff scan the pass and the system validates it
  • You set the price — partner gyms control their own day-pass pricing
  • Weekly payouts — partner gyms are settled to their bank account

Our direction

Vision & Mission

Vision

Access that fits the day

To make training possible wherever you are — a city you're visiting, a week between homes, a schedule that changes — without asking anyone to buy a month they will not use.

Mission

Simpler access, more revenue

To give people a fast, honest way to buy a single day of gym access, and to give gym owners a new stream of revenue from visitors who would otherwise never walk in.

GymHopp was founded by Sukshem K, who started the company in Davanagere, Karnataka, after running into the same problem while travelling: wanting to train for a few days without buying a membership.
