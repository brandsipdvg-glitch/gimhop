About GymHopp

GymHopp is a gym day-pass platform. We let people buy access to a gym for a single day instead of committing to a monthly membership, so a workout no longer depends on how long you happen to be in a city.

Why we built this

While travelling or staying somewhere new, finding a gym for just a few days is surprisingly hard. Most gyms sell monthly memberships, so visitors end up either skipping workouts entirely or paying for a full month they will never use.

We built GymHopp to fix that. You choose a gym and a date, pay for that one day, and receive a QR pass on your phone. You show it at the front desk, staff scan it, and you train. No contract, no negotiation at the counter.

For gym owners, it is revenue that simply does not exist today. These are people who would otherwise never walk through the door.

Founder

GymHopp was founded by Sukshem K, who started the company in Davanagere, Karnataka, after running into the same problem while travelling: wanting to train for a few days without buying a membership.

Sukshem leads the product and partner relationships, working directly with local gym owners as GymHopp launches city by city.

Where we are today

We are onboarding our first partner gyms in Davanagere, Karnataka. Rather than launching with an empty map, we are signing a small number of local gyms first so that early users get real availability from day one.

Booking opens city by city. Join the early-access list on the homepage and we will email you the moment day passes go live near you.

For gym owners

If you run a gym in Davanagere and want additional revenue from short-term visitors, we would like to hear from you. There is no upfront cost, you control your own day-pass pricing, and payouts are settled weekly.
